How to play

Desktop

  • Click to capture the mouse, Esc menu
  • W A S D move, Space jump, Ctrl sprint
  • Space×2 toggle flight (creative), Shift descend
  • Left click break, Right click place / toggle switch
  • 1-9 or wheel select hotbar, Middle click pick block
  • E interact: switches, hubs, wild drones, Gigatron
  • Q swarm command: near a hub assigns a drone, elsewhere recalls all
  • I inventory, H help, T chat (multiplayer), F3 debug

Mobile

  • Left stick moves; drag anywhere else to look
  • Buttons: break, place, E use, Q swarm, jump, descend, fly, menu, inventory

The frontier

  • Biomes: Neon Crystal Plains, Data-Stream Rivers, Circuit Canyons and Floating Sky Islands.
  • Gigatron Beacon: follow the cyan sky-beam. Press E near the guardian for lore and quests.
  • Swarm drones: deploy a Drone Pod or bond with a wild drone (E, costs 1 Neon Crystal). Followers fetch dropped items for you.
  • Hubs & chains: place a Swarm Hub near crystals, press Q beside it to assign a drone; it harvests crystal within 8 blocks. A newer hub within 24 blocks of an older hub links to it and forwards cargo down the chain. Press E on any hub to collect.
  • Energy grid: Power Node (always on) or Switch → Power Wire → Lumen Lamp / Energy Door. Inverter Gate outputs to its sides and top unless the block below it is live. Feed an inverter's output back into its input to make a clock.
  • Saving: autosaves every 30 s to this browser. Share your seed to let friends explore the same terrain.

Support

GIGATRON: Swarm Frontier is free to play, forever. There are no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no wallets and no tracking. Nothing in the game is locked behind payment.

If you enjoy it and want to help, donations are completely optional and change nothing in the game. Other great ways to help: share it with friends, report bugs, or contribute art, quests and code on GitHub.

Multiplayer experimental

Peer-to-peer over WebRTC using the free public PeerJS signaling server (0.peerjs.com). The host's browser runs the world; up to 8 players. Your IP address is visible to the peers you connect with. No accounts.
